Privacy Policy

Goldie respects your privacy and is committed to protecting user data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Goldie mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Goldie collects only the minimum information necessary to operate core application features.

  • Account Information: Users may sign in using Google Authentication. We receive a unique user ID and basic profile information (such as email) required to identify the user account.
  • Wallet & Transactions: The app stores virtual wallet balances and transaction history related to the use of in-app virtual currencies (AGC and SGC).
  • App Preferences: Some settings such as font size, reading progress, Tasbih counts, and user interface preferences may be stored locally on the device to improve the user experience.
  • Notifications: The app may schedule local notifications to remind users about features or updates. These notifications are generated locally on the device and do not require remote push services.

Goldie does not collect contacts, SMS data, call logs, photos, audio recordings, device identifiers, or files from your device.

2. In-App Virtual Currencies

Goldie includes two virtual currencies used exclusively inside the application:

  • AGC (Ancient Gold Coin): A virtual currency purchased through Google Play Billing or earned through certain in-app features such as referrals. AGC can only be used to unlock digital features inside the application. AGC has no real-world monetary value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real money.
  • SGC (Silver Gold Coin): A virtual reward currency earned through participation in app activities such as referrals. SGC can be converted into AGC for in-app use only and also has no real-world monetary value.

3. Payments

All purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing. Goldie does not store or process users’ credit card or payment information. Payment data is handled directly by Google Play according to Google’s privacy policies.

4. Subscriptions

Goldie may offer optional digital subscriptions that unlock premium features within the app. Subscriptions are virtual, non-transferable, and managed securely through Google Play. Subscription status may be verified by the application server to enable or disable features.

5. Third-Party Services

Goldie relies on trusted infrastructure services necessary to operate the application:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Authentication (Google Sign-In)
  • Supabase (backend infrastructure and database services)

These services may process limited information necessary to provide authentication, payment verification, or secure backend functionality according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss. All communication between the app and backend services is encrypted using HTTPS.

7. Data Sharing

Goldie does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. User data is only processed by trusted infrastructure providers necessary to operate the application (such as Supabase and Google Play services).

8. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time. To request account deletion, please visit the following page:

Delete Account Request

After verification, the account and associated data will be permanently deleted, except where retention is required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Goldie may include educational and religious content. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove such information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
